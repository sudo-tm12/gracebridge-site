Common questions

How much is a consultation?

An online consultation costs R500.

Can I get a prescription online?

Yes. Book an online consultation for R500. If the doctor decides medication is appropriate, they issue a prescription, and we prepare and deliver your order.

I already have a prescription. Can I use it?

Yes. Upload a photo or PDF of your prescription. A pharmacist reviews it, confirms availability and price, and arranges delivery.

Do I need a prescription for everything?

No. Over-the-counter items can be ordered directly. Prescription medication needs a valid prescription.

Where do you deliver?

We deliver in selected areas of Johannesburg (northern suburbs including Sandton, Rosebank, Randburg, Fourways, Midrand, Bryanston and Sunninghill) and Pretoria (Pretoria East and Centurion). You can confirm your suburb when you enter your address at checkout.

How much is delivery, and is there a minimum order?

Delivery is a flat R45 per order, and the minimum medication order is R100.

How is my personal information protected?

We process your personal and health information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Read our Privacy Policy.

Privacy and POPIA

Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 November 2026. GraceBridge South Africa (“GraceBridge”, “we”) is the responsible party under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) for the personal information described below.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number and password.
  • Delivery details: your delivery address, suburb, city and postal code.
  • Health information: prescriptions you upload, the reason you give for a consultation, and notes to our pharmacist.
  • Order information: the items you order, order status and payment status. Card details are handled by our payment provider and are not stored by GraceBridge.

2. Why we use it

  • To arrange your online consultation and issue prescriptions.
  • To let a pharmacist review your prescription and prepare your order.
  • To deliver your order and contact you about it.
  • To process payments, prevent fraud and keep our service secure.
  • To meet our legal and professional record-keeping duties.

3. Health information and consent

Health information is special personal information under POPIA. We process it only to provide the service you ask for, and with your consent, which we ask for when you place an order or upload a prescription. You can withdraw your consent at any time, but we may then be unable to complete your order or consultation.

4. Who we share it with

  • Doctors and pharmacists involved in your consultation or order.
  • Delivery partners, who receive only your name, phone number and delivery address.
  • Our payment provider and technology and hosting providers, who process information on our behalf under written agreements.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Storage outside South Africa

Some of our technology providers may store information outside South Africa. Where this happens, we make sure the information stays protected as POPIA requires.

6. Security and breaches

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. If there is a security compromise that affects you, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

7. How long we keep it

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected, or as the law requires, including health and pharmacy record-keeping rules. After that we delete or de-identify it.

Your rights under POPIA

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you and ask for a copy.
  • Ask us to correct or delete information that is inaccurate, out of date or no longer needed.
  • Object to our processing of your information.
  • Withdraw your consent.
  • Opt out of direct marketing at any time.
  • Complain to the Information Regulator.

To use any of these rights, email our Information Officer at privacy@gracebridge.co.za. We will respond within a reasonable time.
